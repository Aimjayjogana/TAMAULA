🔬 How Player Clustering Works

The algorithm groups players based on:

  • Goals scored - Primary attacking contribution
  • Assists provided - Creative contribution
  • Goal contributions - Combined attacking impact
  • Discipline - Yellow/red cards (negative factor)

This reveals natural player types like Goal Scorers, Playmakers, and Supporting Players without any manual labeling.
