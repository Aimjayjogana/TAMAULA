🔬 How Team Clustering Works

Using K-Means unsupervised learning, teams are automatically grouped based on:

✓ Win Rate
Percentage of matches won
✓ Goals Scored/Conceded
Average per match
✓ Squad Size
Number of registered players
✓ Player Quality
Average goals/assists per player

The algorithm finds natural groupings without any pre-defined categories, revealing similar playing styles and performance levels.
